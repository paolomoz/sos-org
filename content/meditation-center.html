Programs & Events

at the International Meditation Center

The Science of Spirituality International Meditation Center offers various programs, workshops and classes.

Since the center’s inauguration in July 2018, tens of thousands have availed of its facilities and the many workshops, retreats and six-week courses that are offered here. In the extended courses, students learn the meditation technique and develop the habit of a daily meditation practice.

Cooking demos are presented by well-known and reputed chefs from around the world. Here, visitors have an opportunity to expand horizons about the benefits of vegetarianism, while also acquiring new cooking skills and recipes.

The center also hosts health symposia each month, with physicians, authors, health experts from all over the country who share research, data and learnings about the importance of a plant based vegetarian diet.

Spiritual Talks with Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj

at the International Meditation Center

On Sunday afternoons, when he is in the Chicagoland area, Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj delivers a spiritual talk in the Main Hall. Attendees to these talks can expect to broaden their spiritual horizons and meet other like-minded individuals who travel from around the world to attend these sessions. All talks are followed by an opportunity to sit in meditation and experience the bliss within.

Meditation Programs and Workshops

at the International Meditation Center

Personal Transformation Through Meditation

Curious about the transformative power of meditation? This class explores the many ways in which meditation can change our lives. The curriculum explores the six qualities of the empowered soul that we can experience as we nourish the soul through meditation.

Learn to Meditate

Whether you are a novice or an experienced meditator, this six-session series will lead you step-by-step to more focused and deeper meditations. In each session, you will have ample time to meditate.

Spiritual Awareness Series

Ever wondered if there is more to this life than meets the eye? Is there more to us than the body and the mind? In this series, we gather to explore the different dimensions of our existence and learn the technique to become aware of our spiritual nature. Come prepared for fascinating glimpses into your true self.

Community Initiatives and Wellness Programs

at the International Meditation Center

Spiritual Book Club

Join us for a Book Study Group in the beautiful library at the Science of Spirituality International Meditation Center. We meet twice monthly on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday of each month to read and discuss books on spirituality and meditation. Each session includes a meditation period. You are welcome to attend each meeting or drop in whenever you can.

Veggie Fest Presents

The Veggie Fest Presents series of programs offers cooking demonstrations, talks on how to transition to a plant-based diet, and celebrity speakers’ events where doctors and specialists provide the latest research on the health benefits of a plant-based diet. Classes are offered in multiple languages.

Yoga

Participants in this yoga class will be guided through a variety of postures to help develop physical flexibility, strength and balance. The class concludes with a short meditation sitting. Bring a yoga mat and wear comfortable clothing.
