Title
Spiritual Quotes & Daily Inspiration
Description
A series of inspiring, spiritual quotes by Sant Rajinder Singh Ji on how meditation fosters inner peace, happiness, and a positive outlook on life.

Spiritual Quotes

When we sit with sincerity and love in our hearts for God, we become receptive to receive what God sends us from within. Truth is one. Whoever comes in contact with God carries back the same truth. The soul has forgotten its true self. It does not know it is a part of God. The soul within us, like God, is consciousness, Light, bliss, and love. Meditation opens our vision to see unity in the diverse fabric of life. Human beings were not made for time, time was made for human beings. When we develop ethical qualities, our spiritual growth comes in leaps and bounds. Anger most hurts the sender. When we realize life’s impermanence, we seek a love that will not end. We have forgotten our connection with God. True happiness comes when we connect with the source of joy within us: our soul and God. The power that moves the body is the power of God. Parents are responsible for instilling the best characteristics in their children. If you don’t go within, you go without. God is always loving, always wise, and always caring. Being receptive is like being an empty cup. This describes the attitude we should adopt in meditation and in our spiritual life. When the soul reunites with God, we are immersed eternally in love and bliss. We are searching for Love all over the world and it lies buried right within us. To achieve union of our soul with God, we must have a yearning for God. Just as a diamond sparkles with light, each soul sparkles with the spark of God. Not a day of our lives should pass without spending some time in meditation. If we dive into the depths of spirituality we will rise high to our spiritual goal of self-knowledge and God-realization. When we meditate, our soul comes in contact with the vibrating principle of God, the current of Light and Sound. In meditation, we should not be disturbed by thoughts generated from our mind. Ups and downs in life are going to happen in the lives of all of us. It is equipoise that we need. The ultimate end of the journey is merging back into God, an infinite ocean of consciousness and love. It is the experience of the soul merging with God which gives us true peace of the soul. And when we achieve this state, it transforms our entire life. Meditation should be an effortless effort. Heal the world, one broken heart at a time. Break through the illusion of the world with the power of meditation. At the level of soul, we are made of the same essence of God, which is love, consciousness, bliss, and Light.
