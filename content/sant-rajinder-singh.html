Meditation Teacher and Spiritual Master

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj (20 September, 1946) is the spiritual head of Science of Spirituality (SOS), known in India as Sawan Kirpal Ruhani Mission (SKRM). With over 3,400 centers in 50 countries, Science of Spirituality has impacted millions of people worldwide.

Coming from a long lineage of Spiritual Masters, Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj has helped countless people embark on the inner spiritual journey and discover the peace and joy that await us within. He is internationally recognized for his work toward promoting inner and outer peace through spirituality.

Teaching an ancient form of meditation, he conveys spirituality as a science that we can all conduct in the laboratory of our bodies to have a practical experience of the Divine. Through this technique, known as SOS Meditation, the practitioner can have a firsthand experience of the inner Light, affirming that there is something that lies beyond this physical world.

Education and Spiritual Mission

Born in India, he completed his spiritual journey under the guidance of two great Spiritual Masters: Sant Kirpal Singh Ji Maharaj (1894–1974) and Sant Darshan Singh Ji Maharaj (1921–1989).

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji earned his B. Tech Degree in Electrical Engineering from the prestigious Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Madras, India, in 1967 and received an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from IIT Chicago, Illinois in 1970, where he was awarded a special IIT Fellowship. He then went on to have a distinguished career in science, computers, and telecommunications. For his accomplishments in the fields of peace and spirituality, he received a Distinguished Leadership Award from IIT Chicago in November 1998.

His training in both disciplines – science and spirituality – has helped him express age-old spiritual teachings in clear, logical language. His spiritual mission began in May 1989 at the direction of his predecessor, Sant Darshan Singh Ji Maharaj. Since then, he has dedicated his life to promoting the many benefits of meditation and assisting countless people embark on the profound, experiential journey of self-discovery to deepen their connection with themselves, God, and their fellow human beings.

Science and spirituality make a great partnership. If those engaged in science spend time in the silence of their selves, inspiration will come and lead them to the answers they seek. If those interested in spirituality apply the scientific law of testing a hypothesis in the laboratories of their own bodies, they will find results. Each person is capable of success in proving spiritual truths. Such pursuits can make the world a better place as well as uncover the purpose of our lives.

– Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj

during an address to students of IIT Mumbai.

Ambassador of Peace and Service to Humanity

Around the world, Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj is known for his visionary leadership in the realm of human unity and integration to bring about inner and outer peace. He emphasizes that building a peaceful world begins with the first foundation stone—ourselves. We must find peace within ourselves before contributing to outer peace.

Over the past three decades, he has convened large global conferences bringing together leaders from all faiths and backgrounds to understand our shared humanity and embrace the commonality of all faiths. These include the International Human Unity Conference held each February and the Global Conference on Mysticism held each September, both in Delhi, India. These conferences draw hundreds of thousands of people from around the world and have been held in Asia, Europe, Africa, and the Americas.

Leading by example, Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj teaches how service to humanity brings meaning to our lives and helps us grow spiritually while making the world a better place. He has inspired and spearheaded countless humanitarian, health, educational, and ecological initiatives around the world. Under his leadership, volunteers at Science of Spirituality centers partner with community organizations to alleviate pain and suffering, and to bring joy to the lives of others.

These activities range from helping victims of natural disasters and conducting vocational training courses to help individuals lift themselves out of poverty, to community-based projects to tackle hunger and chronic disease.

If each person attains inner peace through meditation and develops love for all, it will not be long before peace prevails on our planet. We will live in unity and harmony with each other. In this way, our personal attainment of peace and happiness will contribute to a golden age of peace and happiness on earth.

– Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj

from his best-selling book Inner and Outer Peace through Meditation.

Veggie Fest

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj is the inspiration behind Veggie Fest. One of North America’s largest vegetarian food and wellness festivals, this two-day event, which began in 2005, has drawn over 50,000 people annually.

Championing a nonviolent and compassionate life, which makes it easier for us to grow spiritually, Sant Rajinder Singh Ji is the keynote speaker at Veggie Fest, alongside leading doctors and scientists who promote a whole food, plant-based diet.

A Pioneer in Holistic Education

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj is the Founder and Chairman of Darshan Education Foundation (DEF), which strives to offer students a holistic education through a curriculum focusing on ethical and spiritual development and achieving academic excellence. Under his direction, DEF has established Darshan Academy, a fleet of 23 schools in India and South America that have integrated spirituality into their academic curriculum. Students at Darshan Academies begin their day with meditation and are firmly anchored in the principles of human unity and oneness.
