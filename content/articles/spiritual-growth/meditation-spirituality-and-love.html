Meditation, Spirituality, and Love

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj arrived in Austria on May 25, 2019 for a two-day program of talks and meditation. Wherever he goes, the spiritual Master carries the message that as we learn how to meditate we will enhance our spiritual growth. One of the key benefits of meditation is that it can make our lives an oasis of peace and love amidst the challenges of life.

Spirituality and love

Read more on how to attain peace through meditation in this article.

The spiritual Master says, “Real happiness is  finding joy and contentment in life. Most people, when faced with a terminal disease, no longer care about money. The things that matter to them are their family, what they have accomplished in life, and their faith in God.

These are intangible gifts. Love becomes more important. Love for their family, love for God, and what they did to express their love for humanity are the gifts they take with them into the beyond.”

“Outer loves are but a small reflection of the great love that exists in our soul. If we multiply the greatest love we have known in this outer world by tenfold or a hundredfold, we may have some inkling of the love that awaits us within.”        –Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj
