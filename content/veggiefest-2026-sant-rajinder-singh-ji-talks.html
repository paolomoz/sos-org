Frequently Asked Questions

Do these events cost anything?

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj says spirituality is free. He teaches meditation at no charge, and programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are also free.

How can I learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj's teachings?

Go to www.sos.org to learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj and the Science of Spirituality.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy, and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect, and love for all.

Can I bring my children to the event?

For Aug 8-9 talks at the Veggie Fest main tent, childcare is not available. If you plan to bring your children to the talk, please see an usher for seating.

On Tuesday Aug 11, for the public talk at the Science of Spirituality Meditation Center there will be childcare for children 12 and under that will run concurrent with the public talk. For children under the age of 6 on August 11, one parent must stay with their child in the children’s area. See SOS registration upon arrival for more information.

How can I learn more about local and on-line programs in my area?

Visit https://www.sos.org/find-local-programs/. Enter your location in the search box to find upcoming local events. And click on “Find Online Programs” to view a list of upcoming online programs.

What is Veggie Fest?

Veggie Fest is one of the largest food festivals in North America. Entry is free. To find out more about Veggie Fest, visit https://veggiefestchicago.org

Is there parking available at the Science of Spirituality International Meditation Center?

There is ample parking available at the center.

Where do I park at Veggie Fest for the talks on Aug 8 and Aug 9?

Convenient and security patrolled, FREE parking is available near the festival at Navistar, 2701 Navistar Circle, Lisle, IL 60532.

What are in-depth meditation instructions?

Many people first discover meditation because of its physical and mental benefits. Others find that their search naturally deepens into a spiritual one.

In-Depth Meditation Instructions are for those who wish to explore meditation as a spiritual practice rather than simply a wellness practice.

These instructions introduce a meditation method that helps us recognize our true spiritual nature and begin experiencing the Divine within.

For those who feel called to continue this journey, it is an invitation to explore life’s deepest purpose through personal experience.

“Meditation is a private retreat from the problems of the world.”
