Sawan Kirpal Ruhani Mission (SKRM) - India

Sawan Kirpal Ruhani Mission (SKRM) is the name under which Science of Spirituality is known in India. It carries forward a spiritual legacy centered on meditation on the inner Light and Sound, love for all humanity, ethical living, and selfless service.

Through spiritual guidance, humanitarian outreach, educational initiatives, and large public programs, SKRM has touched the lives of countless people across India.

Rooted in the teachings of the saints, SKRM helps people from all walks of life discover the spiritual treasures within themselves while inspiring them to live lives of compassion, peace, and service. It serves the community through a range of initiatives, nurturing spiritual growth and supporting education, healthcare support, and humanitarian needs.

A Strong Presence Across India

SKRM has centers across India—close to 3,000 and counting—where people gather for meditation, spiritual discourse, satsang, fellowship, and service activities. These centers serve as places of inner reflection and community support, helping seekers deepen their spiritual lives in an atmosphere of peace, devotion, and love.

Major International Conferences

Among the most significant SKRM activities in India are two major annual international convenings that draw widespread participation and attention. Together, these conferences reach millions of people through participation, outreach, and the widespread sharing of their message.

International Human Unity Conference

Held in February, the International Human Unity Conference emphasizes the oneness of all humanity and the values needed for a peaceful world. It brings together people from many backgrounds to reflect on harmony, understanding, and spiritual upliftment.

Global Conference on Mysticism

Held in September, the Global Conference on Mysticism explores the universal mystic traditions that point to inner spiritual awakening. It attracts broad participation and inspires seekers with teachings on meditation, inner experience, and divine love.

Service to Humanity and Outreach

SKRM’s spiritual mission is inseparable from service to humanity. Across India, the organization regularly carries out humanitarian activities that bring practical help and hope to people in need.

Its outreach efforts extend care and assistance to communities on a broader scale, while also introducing people to practical spirituality, the value of meditation, and the principles of unity, love, and peaceful living.

Education and Darshan Academy

Education is another important pillar of SKRM’s work in India. Through Darshan Academy schools, the organization supports the development of young people with an emphasis on academic excellence, ethical values, compassion, and spiritual awareness. This educational work helps nurture responsible, caring, and balanced future generations.
