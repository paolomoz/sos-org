Frequently Asked Questions

Do these events cost anything?

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj says spirituality is free. He teaches meditation at no charge, and programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are also free.

How can I learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj&apos;s teachings?

Go to www.sos.org to learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj and the Science of Spirituality.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy, and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect, and love for all.

How do I get to the Hyatt Regency Seattle Hotel?

Travel to Hyatt Regency via Sound Transit Light Rail from Seattle Airport; fare is $3.00. Exit at downtown at Westlake Station, hotel is a three-block walk. Travel time: 40 minutes.

Where is the nearest parking at the hotel?

There is a discounted $35.00 daily/event self-parking (up to 8 hours) based on availability in hotel parking garage.

Will there be provisions for child care during the programs?

Yes – there will be a designated location at the Center for children’s activities for kids 6 years and older, during the talks by Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj. For kids younger than 6 years, a parent/guardian must stay with them. Upon arrival, register your child at the registration desk, and details will be made available.
