In a world of constant noise, stress, and uncertainty, how can we find lasting peace? Join Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj, an internationally respected spiritual Master and global meditation teacher, for an illuminating talk on tapping into our spiritual core, followed by detailed meditation instructions.

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji will share a simple yet profound meditation technique that has helped over a million people experience deep inner stillness and clarity. Learn how to tap into your own source of peace — a sanctuary untouched by external turmoil, and how that inner calm can ripple outward to bring harmony to our homes, communities, and world.

Come experience timeless spiritual insight, practical tools for modern life, and the transformative power of meditation that promotes physical health, reduces stress and anxiety, and helps the individual aspirant attain higher consciousness through tapping into one’s spiritual core.

All programs are free of charge and open to all.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these events cost anything?

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj says spirituality is free. He teaches meditation at no charge, and programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are also free.

How can I learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj&apos;s teachings?

Go to www.sos.org to learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj and the Science of Spirituality.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect and love for all.

How do I get to the Lake Mary Mariott Hotel?

The event is being held at the Lake Mary Marriott Hotel – located just west of I-4 at Exit 101A in Lake Mary, less than 20 minutes from downtown Orlando.

Directions can be found here

Where is the nearest parking at the hotel?

Lake Mary Mariott Hotel has free parking available for those coming to attend the event

Will there be provisions for child care during the programs?

Yes – there will be a room designated at the hotel for child care for kids 6 years and older, during the talks by Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj. For kids less than 6 years, a parent/guardian must stay with them but there will be a live feed to Sant Rajinder Singh’s talk in the children’s room.
