Frequently Asked Questions

Do these events cost anything?

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj says spirituality is free. He teaches meditation at no charge, and programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are also free.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy, and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect, and love for all.

Can I bring my children to the event?

For Aug 9-10 talks at the Veggie Fest main tent, childcare is not available. If you plan to bring your children to the talk, please see an usher for seating.

On Tuesday Aug 12, for the public talk at the Science of Spirituality Meditation Center there will be childcare for children 12 and under that will run concurrent with the public talk. For children under the age of 6 on August 12, one parent must stay with their child in the children’s area. See SOS registration upon arrival for more information.

How can I learn more about local and on-line programs in my area?

Visit https://www.sos.org/find-local-programs/. Enter your location in the search box to find upcoming local events. And click on “Find Online Programs” to view a list of upcoming online programs.

What is Veggie Fest?

Veggie Fest is one of the largest food festivals in North America. Entry is free. To find out more about Veggie Fest, visit https://veggiefestchicago.org

Is there parking available at the Science of Spirituality International Meditation Center?

There is ample parking available at the center.

Where do I park at Veggie Fest for the talks on Aug 9 and Aug 10?

Convenient and security patrolled, FREE parking is available near the festival at Navistar, 2701 Navistar Circle, Lisle, IL 60532.
