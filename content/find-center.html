Science of Spirituality (SOS) Meditation Locations

Science of Spirituality (SOS) is a global nonprofit with more than 3,500 locations worldwide—each a space for meditation, inner growth and connection. Wherever you are on your journey, there’s a welcoming SOS community nearby.

Our locations fall into two categories:

SOS Meditation Center

SOS Meditation Centers are dedicated facilities that host meditation retreats, wellness talks, spiritual programs, and more.

SOS Meditation Community

SOS Meditation community or local chapters are smaller gatherings in homes or community spaces offering regular satsangs (talks) and meditation sessions.
