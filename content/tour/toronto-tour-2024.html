Frequently Asked Questions

Do these events cost anything?

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj says spirituality is free. He teaches meditation at no charge, and programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are also free.

How can I learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji's teachings?

Go to www.sos.org to learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji and the Science of Spirituality.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy, and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect, and love for all.

Where is the nearest parking for this event?

Parking is available at the Delta Hotels Toronto Airport. The reduced daily parking rate for attendees is $10.00 CAD. Please see the hotel reception for a special Science of Spirituality (SOS) parking coupon upon arrival.

Transportation

The hotel is conveniently accessible by various modes of transport and there is a TTC bus stop right in front of the hotel. The hotel is a 12 minutes drive from Kipling Transit Terminal. Shuttles operate between the airport and the hotels every 30 minutes.

Children's Programs

On June 29th and 30th, children’s programs for kids 12 and under will run concurrently with the talks. For children under the age of six, one parent is asked to stay with their child in the children’s area. See SOS registration upon arrival for more information.
