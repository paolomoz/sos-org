Frequently Asked Questions

Do these events cost anything?

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj says spirituality is free. He teaches meditation at no charge, and programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are also free.

How can I learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji's teachings?

Go to www.sos.org to learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji and the Science of Spirituality.

What does Sant Rajinder Singh Ji mean by “Detox the Mind”?

Just like the physical body, the mind can also be overwhelmed by toxins such as hatred, anger, jealousy, and other negative tendencies that interfere with our peace of mind. By uprooting these from our life, we can begin to experience the peace, joy, love, and bliss that are already inherent within us.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy, and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect, and love for all.

Where is the nearest parking for this event?

There is no parking at the SOS Amityville Meditation Center on Saturday, May 20 or Sunday, May, 21. Arrangements have been made for attendees to park at the LIRR Amityville Train Station, North Parking Lot #1. From there, there will be regular free SOS Shuttles to take attendees from North Lot #1 to the Center, returning after the program. Attendees can also come to the Center (via Merrick Road heading north on County Line Road) to drop off passengers, and then park at the Station. Click here for map of the area.
