About Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj is the spiritual head of Science of Spirituality. A world renowned spiritual Master, he has dedicated his life to teaching meditation to help people discover their inner treasures. Trained as a scientist, he presents spirituality as a science that can be practiced by people of all backgrounds.

Author of several best selling books & a keynote speaker at various conferences & academic institutions including the United Nations. Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj travels tirelessly around the world, to share his message of love, unity and peace.

There is free parking available at the Hindu Temple temple of Greater Chicago.

Can children attend this event?

Children 6 years and older are welcome to attend the program.

A designated area will be available for children under 6, where they can stay with at least one parent for the duration of the program.

Yes, complimentary boxed meals will be distributed to all attendees after the event.

What is the dress code for this event?

How can I learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji's teachings?

Go to www.sos.org to learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji and the Science of Spirituality.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy, and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect, and love for all.
