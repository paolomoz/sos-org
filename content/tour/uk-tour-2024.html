Frequently Asked Questions

Do these events cost anything?

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj says spirituality is free. He teaches meditation at no charge, and programmes sponsored by Science of Spirituality are also free.

How can I learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj's teachings?

Go to www.sos.org to learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj and the Science of Spirituality.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect and love for all.

How can I get to the International Convention Centre (ICC)?

By Train: The ICC is conveniently located within walking distance of three train stations in Birmingham city center, with New Street Station being the nearest.

By Foot: Exit New Street Station and head towards Victoria Square. Follow the signs for Centenary Square, ICC, and Utilita Arena Birmingham. Once in Centenary Square, walk straight ahead, passing the iconic Library of Birmingham and the Rep Theatre on your right. The ICC will be directly in front of you.

By Car: The ICC is conveniently located at the heart of the UK’s motorway network, with easy access from the M6, M5, and M42.

By Air: Birmingham Airport offers convenient transport links to the city center. A train station is directly connected to the terminal, providing a quick 10-minute journey to Birmingham New Street. Taxis are also readily available outside the airport.

Further details are available on the ICC website.

How can I get to the InterContinental London - The O2?

By Tube: The nearest London Underground station is North Greenwich station, serviced by the Jubilee Line.

By Car: If you are driving, please use postcode SE10 0TW.

Further details are available on the InterContinental London – O2 website.

Where is the nearest parking for this event?

Birmingham: Parking is available near the International Convention Centre at the Utilita Arena (King Edward’s Road, B1 2AA). Mobile payments can be made upon arrival at the Arena car park via www.parkjockey.com/pay, quoting location code 5020.
Visit the Utilita Arena website (https://www.utilitaarenabham.co.uk/your-visit/parking/) for further details on parking options and potential pre-booking availability.

London: Limited on-site parking is available at the InterContinental London – The O2 on a first-come, first-served basis. The cost is £22 for 4-6 hours and must be paid at the hotel reception. Different tariffs apply for longer stays for hotel or conference guests. Please check with the hotel reception.
Parking apps like Parkopedia, JustPark, or YourParkingSpace can be used to find and book alternative parking options in advance.

We recommend checking for real-time parking availability and pricing options close to your chosen location before your trip.

How do I get from Birmingham to London?

By train: The average train journey from London to Birmingham is approximately 2 hours and 3 minutes. There are around 173 trains operating daily on this route. For the most up-to-date timetables and ticket options, please visit the Trainline website or app.

By car: The most direct route to London from Birmingham is via the M40 motorway. However, alternative options include the M1 and M25. Expect a driving time of approximately 2-3 hours, depending on traffic conditions.

 For real-time traffic updates and to find the best route for your journey, use a traffic service like Google Maps or Waze.

Will there be provisions for childcare during the programmes?

A programme for children under 13 will run concurrently with the talks. For children under the age of five, one parent is asked to stay with their child in the children’s area. See SOS registration upon arrival for more information.
