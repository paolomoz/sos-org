About Us

Science of Spirituality is a worldwide, spiritual organization dedicated to transforming lives, under the guidance of renowned spiritual Master Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj. Science of Spirituality presents spirituality as a science that can be practiced by people of all backgrounds and faiths.
