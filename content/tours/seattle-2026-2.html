Is there more to life?
Beneath our many desires lies a deeper longing, a longing to reconnect with Divine Source from which we came.

Join spiritual Master Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj for an exploration of life’s deepest questions and how we can begin the journey to experience the Divine for ourselves.

Whether you are exploring spirituality for the first time or have been seeking answers for many years, this event offers a rare opportunity to hear from a spiritual Master whose teachings have inspired millions around the world.

Come discover why people of all ages, from diverse backgrounds, cultures, and faith traditions have been drawn to Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj and his message.

In the hustle and bustle of daily life, we often search for peace in the world around us, forgetting that the true source of peace resides within.

In this profound talk, world-renowned spiritual Master, Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj, will guide us through the transformative power of meditation. By learning to quiet the mind, we unlock a gateway to deeper consciousness and a lasting sense of inner peace.

What to Expect

The public talk begins at 3:00 PM and is expected to conclude at approximately 4:00 PM, including a brief meditation sitting.

Guests who would like to continue are welcome to stay afterward for detailed meditation instructions into the inner Light and Sound, followed by complimentary boxed snacks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these events cost anything?

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj says spirituality is free. He teaches meditation at no charge, and programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are also free.

How can I learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj's teachings?

Go to www.sos.org to learn more about Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj and the Science of Spirituality.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy, and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect, and love for all.

How do I get to the Hyatt Regency Seattle Hotel?

Travel to the Hyatt Regency via Sound Transit Light Rail from Seattle Airport; the fare is $3.00. Exit at Westlake Station downtown; the hotel is a three-block walk away. Travel time: 40 minutes.

Where is the nearest parking at the hotel?

There is a discounted $35.00 daily/event self-parking fee (up to 8 hours), subject to availability, in the hotel parking garage.

Will there be provisions for child care during the programs?

Yes – there will be a designated location at the venue for children’s activities for kids 6 years and older, during the talks by Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj. For children younger than 6 years of age, a parent/guardian must stay with them. Upon arrival, please register your child at the registration desk, where additional details will be provided.
