Meditation in everyday life

Saturday, August 30, 2025, 4:00 p.m.

Join us for an uplifting talk by Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj, a world-renowned spiritual Master, as he shares how meditation can be seamlessly woven into the fabric of our daily lives.

Discover how this simple yet profound practice helps you stay centered amid life’s challenges, brings clarity to your decisions, and nurtures lasting peace and well-being. Through meditation, learn to shift from stress and distraction to a life of presence, purpose, and inner strength.

Whether you’re taking your first steps or deepening your practice, this talk will offer practical insights to make meditation a natural part of your everyday journey.

Happiness begins in silence

Sunday, August 31, 2025, 3:00 p.m.

BOK Sportcsarnok (Budapest Olimpiai Központ)
Budapest, Dózsa György út 1, 1146 Hungary

True happiness isn’t found in the noise of the world—it begins in the silence within.

Join us for a heart-opening talk by Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj, a world-renowned spiritual Master, as he explains how the practice of inner silence through meditation can awaken lasting joy, peace, and clarity in our lives.

In a world filled with constant distractions, this talk offers a refreshing reminder that the key to fulfillment lies not in chasing the external, but in discovering the stillness of our soul. Learn how to access that silence and uncover the happiness that’s always been within you.

All programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are free of charge and open to all.
