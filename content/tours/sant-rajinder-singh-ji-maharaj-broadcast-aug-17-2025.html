Join us for an inspiring and transformative evening as world-renowned Spiritual Master and expert teacher of meditation, Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj, shares profound insights on how meditation can lead to deep spiritual wellness, inner peace, and a more balanced life.

This live broadcast will be streamed in real-time across all the Science of Spirituality Meditation Centers in North and Latin America, bringing together tens of thousands of participants in a powerful, collective experience of unity, peace, and self-discovery.

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj teaches a simple, yet effective form of meditation designed to promote physical health, reduce stress, and enhance spiritual awareness. During this program he will provide detailed meditation instructions, followed by a short period of meditation.

Whether you’re new to meditation or seeking to deepen your practice, this special event offers tools to uplift your soul and nourish your heart.

Event Details:

  • A live broadcast talk by Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj
  • Group meditation practice
  • Community gathering at your nearest SOS center
  • Free vegetarian meal or refreshments
  • Uplifting atmosphere of love, reflection, and oneness

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a live broadcast event?

Yes, this is a special live broadcast event where Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj’s talk from Chicago will be streamed to meditation centers across North and South America. It’s a rare and beautiful opportunity to experience his uplifting message right in your own city, while joining a heartfelt community gathering at your local Science of Spirituality center.

Do these events cost anything?

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj says spirituality is free. He teaches meditation at no charge, and programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are also free.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation has been proven to reduce stress and create greater peace, happiness, empathy, and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, they will treat others with kindness, respect, and love for all. Societies will begin to reflect this enhanced sense of harmony and mutual understanding.

Can I bring my children to the event?

Yes, there will be a dedicated space for children 12 and under during the public talk. For children under 6, we kindly ask that one parent remain with them in the children’s area. Please visit the registration desk when you arrive for more details.

How can I learn more about local and on-line programs in my area?

Visit https://www.sos.org/find-local-programs/. Enter your location in the search box to find upcoming local events. And click on “Find Online Programs” to view a list of upcoming online programs.

Is there parking available?

For parking information, please contact the specific center you plan to attend. Contact details are listed in the “Centers” section above.
