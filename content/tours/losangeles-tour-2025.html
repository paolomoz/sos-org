In a world of constant noise, stress, and uncertainty, how can we find lasting peace? Join Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj, an internationally respected spiritual Master and global meditation teacher, for an illuminating talk on discovering the calm within amidst the chaos without.

Sant Rajinder SIngh Ji will share a simple yet profound meditation technique that has helped countless thousands experience deep inner stillness and clarity. Learn how to tap into your own source of peace — a sanctuary untouched by external turmoil, and how that inner calm can ripple outward to bring harmony to our homes, communities, and world.

Come experience timeless spiritual insight, practical tools for modern life, and the transformative power of meditation to awaken peace and calm within and around you.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect and love for all.
