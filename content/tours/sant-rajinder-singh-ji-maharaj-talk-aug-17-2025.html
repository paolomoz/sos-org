Sunday, August 17

Meditation – A Path to Spiritual Wellness

17 August 2025 – 3:00 PM

International Meditation Center – 4105 Naperville Rd, Lisle, IL

Register Now

Join us for an inspiring and transformative evening as world-renowned Spiritual Master and expert teacher of meditation, Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj, shares profound insights on how meditation can lead to deep spiritual wellness, inner peace, and a more balanced life.

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj teaches a simple, yet effective form of meditation designed to promote physical health, reduce stress, and enhance spiritual awareness. During this program he will provide detailed meditation instructions, followed by a short period of meditation.

Whether you’re new to meditation or seeking to deepen your practice, this special event offers tools to uplift your soul and nourish your heart.

This live broadcast will be streamed in real-time across North and Latin America, bringing together tens of thousands of participants in a powerful, collective experience of unity, silence, and self-discovery.

All programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are free of charge and open to all.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these events cost anything?

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj says spirituality is free. He teaches meditation at no charge, and programs sponsored by Science of Spirituality are also free.

How can meditation lead to both inner and outer peace?

Meditation is proven to reduce stress and helps create a general feeling of peace, happiness, empathy, and clarity. As more and more people experience these benefits, it won’t be long before society begins to operate with kindness, respect, and love for all.

Can I bring my children to the event?

We’re happy to offer childcare for kids 12 and under during the public talk! Parents of children under 6, please plan to stay with your little ones in the children’s area. More details available at registration when you arrive.

How can I learn more about local and on-line programs in my area?

To find local events in Chicagoland area, visit https://www.sos.org/lisle. For a list of upcoming online programs, click on “Programs” and “Online Programs” from the top navigation menu. You can select from of the available series of online events.

Is there parking available at the Science of Spirituality International Meditation Center?

There is ample parking available at the center.

Will there be food served after the program?

Yes, a complimentary vegetarian meal will be served after the program. All are welcome to stay and enjoy the food and warm community atmosphere.
