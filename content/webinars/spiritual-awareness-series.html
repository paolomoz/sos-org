Why this Workshop

Spiritual Awareness Series

The curriculum of the Spiritual Awareness Series embraces a wide range of topics designed to guide you on your spiritual journey.

The variety of topics covered in the Spiritual Awareness series include introduction to the SOS Meditation technique, practical strategies for addressing some of the most common challenges of meditation, how to tap into the qualities of our empowered soul to lead a spiritually and emotionally rich and fulfilling life. Central to each is learning how to deepen our meditation practice.

Although each class stands on its own, we recommend you attend the entire series to fully access the meaningful life-changing content.

About Us

Science of Spirituality is a worldwide, spiritual organization dedicated to transforming lives through meditation, under the guidance of renowned spiritual Master Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj.

Science of Spirituality presents spirituality as a science that can be practiced by people of all backgrounds and faiths.
