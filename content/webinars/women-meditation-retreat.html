Divine Beauty – A Women’s Retreat

‘Divine Beauty, Creating your Authentic Self’, is a series of four distinct workshops designed for women’s self-empowerment. Meditation is the key that unlocks the inner treasures of unconditional love, unlimited wisdom, and inner peace.

All four workshops highlight how meditation can enhance all aspects of life. Whether you are just starting your life’s journey, a mother raising children, in the workforce, or a senior in her golden years, Divine Beauty has something for you. Join us for this free online interactive webinar.
