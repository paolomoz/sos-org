Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj Events

Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj is a world-renowned meditation teacher, acclaimed author, and respected public speaker on meditation, spirituality, and personal transformation. He is the head of Science of Spirituality, a global nonprofit organization.

He teaches a simple and effective technique of meditation on the inner Light and Sound, which can be practiced by people of all faiths and backgrounds. Traveling extensively around the world, he shares universal spiritual teachings that help people experience inner peace, deeper clarity, and a greater sense of purpose.

His talks offer practical guidance on how to overcome life’s challenges and live a more fulfilling, balanced, and compassionate life through meditation.

Through these free public talks—offered both in person and online—participants can learn this meditation technique and explore a more peaceful, purposeful way of living.
