Spiritual Inspirations, Podcasts by Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj

Welcome to Spiritual Inspirations. In this series of weekly podcasts offered by Science of Spirituality, internationally known Spiritual Master Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj speaks about coping with the challenges in our lives. Each week he will focus on a topical issue, explaining how meditation is the key to being able to calmly navigate through difficult times.

The uplifting message the Spiritual Master carries all over the globe is we can realize the power of our soul through meditation and access the love and peace within us, healing ourselves and the world.
